Prayer
Closing Prayer
Lord, you scattered Babel because its unity was built on pride and the erasure of difference.

You answered at Pentecost not by restoring one language, but by speaking every language at once — because you have always loved the particular.

You broke down the dividing wall not to make us all the same, but to make us one body in our difference.

Give us the courage to be a social miracle — a community that the world cannot explain, because you alone are our peace.

In the name of the one who gathers every tribe, tongue, and nation around his throne. Amen.
