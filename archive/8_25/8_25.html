Ordinary Glory  ·  Bible Study — August 25

The Good Life: Receive It, Share It, Enjoy It

Aa

All Questions

Part 1 — Receive Your Life
1
Brother David says every moment is a gift — we did not buy it or earn it. Think about yesterday. What is one moment you can now call a “gift” from God?
2
Mary Oliver says “Attention is the beginning of devotion.” What good things in your life do you rush through instead of noticing?
Part 2 — Give and Receive  ·  John 13:6–8
3
Peter did not want Jesus to wash his feet. Why is it sometimes harder to let someone serve you than to serve someone else?
4
Bonhoeffer says the Christ in our own heart is weaker than the Christ we hear in the words of a brother or sister. How has another believer helped you hear from God?
5
Lilla Watson says real help happens when we know our future is tied to each other, not just when one person helps another from far away. How is your life connected to the people around you?
Part 3 — Don’t Live It Alone  ·  Ecclesiastes 4:7–12
6
“Two are better than one” and “a cord of three strands is not quickly broken.” Who are the two or three people who hold you up when life is hard?
7
Jesus sent his disciples out two by two, not alone. C.S. Lewis reminds us that every person we meet matters to God. Who in your life needs a companion right now, not just advice?
8
The first church shared teaching, fellowship, meals, prayer, and even their money together. Which of these is weak in your life right now, and which one could you grow in?
Part 4 — Enjoy Your Life  ·  Ecclesiastes 9:7–9
9
Joy shows that God is near, and it can even fight against despair. What steals your joy the most? What simple gift from God can you enjoy today?
10
Solomon says: eat, drink, dress well, and enjoy your life — don’t wait. Is there a joy you keep putting off until “someday”? What could you do this week instead?

Closing
Prayer
Closing Prayer
Heavenly Father,

Thank you for this day — it is a gift we did not earn. Help us to receive it, instead of rushing past it.

Help us to open the bottle: to share the good things you have given us, and not keep them only for ourselves.

Help us to break the bread: to eat together, to sit with people, and to not carry our joy alone.

Help us to do good work with our hands, whatever that work may be, as an offering back to you.

Help us to love your people — to give when we are able, and to also let others give to us.

And Father, remind us every day: don’t do any of it alone. Walk with us, and give us people to walk beside.

We ask this in the name of Jesus, who gave us his own life so that we could receive it. Amen.
— Based on the closing lines of this week’s study: Receive the gift, open the bottle, break the bread, do good work, love your people, and don’t do any of it alone.
